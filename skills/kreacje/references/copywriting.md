# Copywriting

On-screen text is read in one to three seconds, often without sound. Every word has to earn its place.

## Language and voice

- One language per draft: the brief's; else the brand channel language; else Polish.
- Voice: follow the brand profile (tone, rules and don'ts, words to avoid) and how the brand writes in its knowledge documents. Unknown voice: plain, warm and direct, addressing the viewer in the second person singular (in Polish: "Sprawdź", "Zamów").
- Product, service and place names exactly as the brief or the profile writes them.

## Length and structure

- One idea per scene. Concrete nouns and stated facts beat adjectives.
- Stay within the field's `recommended_max_chars` for the draft's format; `max_length` is a hard limit. Shorten by rewriting, never by cutting a word or adding an ellipsis.
- Hook: the benefit or the news in about six words. No greeting, no "Zobacz nasz film".
- Supporting lines (subline, caption): add one fact the headline does not say.
- CTA: an imperative verb plus what and where ("Zamów na <adres z briefu>", "Zarezerwuj stolik"). Contact data, addresses and URLs only from the brief or the profile; a discount code goes only into its own field (`regulated-values.md`).
- Lists: parallel items of similar length, no full stops at the end.

## What not to write

- Claims nobody can check: "najlepszy", "nr 1", "gwarancja", health, eco or origin claims, unless the brief or the profile states them.
- Invented facts: numbers of customers, years on the market, awards, ratings, quotes.
- Prices, old prices, discounts, percentages, discount codes and promotion dates or deadlines in any free text (headline, kicker, subline, caption, CTA, list item, text overlay, scene title), even when the brief states them: they go only into their regulated fields, which the user confirms before the render (`regulated-values.md`). Write "Zimowe kurtki taniej", not "-20% na kurtki do niedzieli".
- Hashtags; emojis unless the brand uses them; more than one exclamation mark per draft; whole sentences in capitals (layouts capitalise where they need to).

## Polish typography

- Sentence case: "Nowa kolekcja na zimę", never "Nowa Kolekcja Na Zimę".
- Quotation marks „…”.
- A space between a number and its unit or currency: "199 zł", "30 dni", "2 godziny".
- A spaced en dash " – " for a pause, not a hyphen.
- No full stop at the end of a single-line headline or a CTA.
- Dates and times as people write them: "29 listopada", "29.11", "18:00". Regulated dates stay exactly as the brief writes them.

English copy: sentence case, the same brevity, no Title Case headlines.
